# Voice samples

Drop recordings here for the game's cloned voice. Recordings here
are published with the games, so only record voices that may be online.

## How to record
1. Use a phone voice memo app in a quiet room (no TV, fan, or music).
2. Hold the phone about a hand's length from your mouth.
3. Read ONE of the sentences below at a normal conversational pace — not slowed
   down for the kids. The cloned voice copies your pace, and the model expects
   ordinary speed. Sentence A should take about 4–6 seconds.
4. Do 2–3 takes. Leave a half-second of silence at the start and end.
5. Save/AirDrop the files into this folder. Any format is fine (.m4a, .wav, .mp3).
   Name them like: ma_take1.m4a, ma_take2.m4a

## Sentences to read (cover all the tones)
A. Hôm nay má dạy con đọc bài. Con mở sách ra, đọc to và rõ nha.
B. Mỗi buổi sáng, ba chở con đi học bằng xe đạp.

Only record voices of adults who are OK being the game's voice.
